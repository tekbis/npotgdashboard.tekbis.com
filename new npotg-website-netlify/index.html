FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What services can you provide at home?

Wellness exams and everyday primary care, IV therapy and vitamin injections, medical weight loss, pain management, sclerotherapy, Botox and dermal fillers. If you're not sure whether something can be done at home, send a request and we'll let you know.

Is it safe to have treatments at home?

Yes. Every visit is performed by a licensed, board-certified nurse practitioner using sterile, single-use supplies and the same clinical protocols as an office visit, with emergency equipment on hand.

How soon will I see Botox results?

Neurotoxin starts working in 3–5 days with full results at about two weeks. Results typically last 3–4 months.

Meet Your Nurse Practitioner

Medical care and aesthetics, from one provider

I'm a board-certified family nurse practitioner, and I started Nurse Practitioner On The Go to care for the whole person. That means your everyday health and your aesthetic goals, from the same trusted provider.

Some visits are about wellness: an annual exam, a sick visit, IV hydration, a weight-loss plan or relief from pain. Others are about looking and feeling refreshed with Botox, fillers or sclerotherapy. Every one gets the same clinical care, and it all happens in the comfort of your own space.
